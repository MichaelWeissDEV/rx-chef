# A1Z26 Cipher Encode

## Overview

Converts alphabet characters into their corresponding alphabet order number.<br><br>e.g. <code>a</code> becomes <code>1</code> and <code>b</code> becomes <code>2</code>.<br><br>Non-alphabet characters are dropped.
