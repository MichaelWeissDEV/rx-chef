# LZMA Compress

Compresses data using the Lempel–Ziv–Markov chain algorithm. Compression mode determines the speed and effectiveness of the compression: 1 is fastest and less effective, 9 is slowest and most effective

- Input: `Bytes`
- Output: `Bytes`
- CLI: `rxchef run "LZMA Compress"`

## Arguments

| # | Argument | Default | Description |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | Compression Mode | `7` | Compression mode determines the speed and effectiveness of the compression: 1 is fastest and less effective, 9 is slowest and most effective |
